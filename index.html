Welcome to the woods

A place to stay, help, wander, and connect.

Elfendahl Forest is an evolving homestead community where the practical and the playful share the same patch of earth. Visitors may encounter an outdoor kitchen, working gardens, friendly animals, solar-powered projects, forest roads, and creative surprises tucked around the property.

Some people arrive for a campsite. Others come to lend a hand, share a meal, or spend time outdoors. However you visit, expect a real, lived-in community—not a polished resort—and bring your curiosity.

Lend a hand

Volunteer stays

Volunteers contribute four hours of work per day in exchange for a free campsite and community potluck dinners.

Work changes with the needs of the property and may include caring for shared spaces, preparing campsites, helping in the garden, feeding animals, handing tools, or contributing a useful skill to an active project.

Volunteer stays are arranged directly and depend on current needs and availability.

A day might include

  • Four hours of useful, agreed-upon help
  • Time to rest or explore afterward
  • A campsite in the forest
  • A community potluck dinner

Know before you go

Plan for a comfortable visit.

Where is Elfendahl Forest?

The community is near Belfair, Washington. Confirm the exact location and arrival instructions through your booking platform or directly with the host before traveling.

How do I reserve a campsite?

Choose a rental above to book through Airbnb, or explore the Hipcamp listing. Elfendahl Forest does not operate a separate reservation system on this website.

What should I expect?

This is an active homestead and sustainable living community. Amenities, policies, access, seasonal conditions, and animal considerations should be confirmed in the current listing before you book.

Can I volunteer?

Volunteer stays may be available when there is suitable work and space. Contact the community directly to discuss timing, needs, expectations, and fit.
